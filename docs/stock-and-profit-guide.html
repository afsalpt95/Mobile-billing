Stock & Profit Guide

My Mobile Shop POS: adding products, adding stock, unit cost, IMEI, stock movements, adjustments and how each one affects Profit & Loss.

1The big picture

Every product that keeps stock has two numbers: quantity in stock and average cost (what one unit cost you on average). Everything else in this guide changes one or both of those numbers.

Stock comes inOpening stock, Purchase, Stock-in adjustment, customer return
→
ProductQty in stock + average cost (recalculated on every stock-in)
→
Stock goes outSale, repair spare used, lost / damaged adjustment, vendor return
→
Profit & LossSales − cost of goods sold − write-offs − expenses

Each of these steps writes one line to Stock movements, a permanent history of every stock change. Lines are never edited or deleted. Mistakes are fixed by adding a new line.

Key ruleBuying stock is not an expense. A purchase turns money into stock. The cost only reaches Profit & Loss when you sell the item (as cost of goods sold) or lose/damage it (as a write-off).

2How to add a product

  1. Go to Stock in the top menu, then click + Add product (top right).
  2. Fill in the fields (explained in the table below). Only Product name, Type and Selling price are required.
  3. Decide on Track IMEI / serial numbers for each unit. See section 3. Get this right now, because it can only be changed later while stock is 0.
  4. If you already have this item on the shelf, fill in the Opening stock box at the bottom: quantity, unit cost and, for IMEI items, one IMEI per unit.
  5. Click Save & select.
The Opening stock box only appears when creating a product.It is not shown on the Edit screen. If you save with 0 opening stock, you have to add stock another way (see section 4).

3Track IMEI: when to use it

With IMEI tracking on, the app follows every single unit by its IMEI/serial number, not just a count.

✅ Turn it ON for

Phones, tablets, smart watches, laptops: any item where each unit has its own serial number and you care which exact unit was sold (warranty, theft checks, returns).

❌ Keep it OFF for

Back covers, tempered glass, cables, chargers, earphones without serials, spare parts, consumables. Otherwise you'd have to type a fake serial for every unit you buy and sell.

My product already exists with 0 stock. How do I add opening stock?

The Edit screen has no opening stock box. Your options, best first:

  1. If it was bought from a supplier: record a Purchase from that vendor with the real qty and cost, and enter the amount you paid. This is correct in every report.
  2. If it's genuinely old stock and the product has no sales yet: deactivate the product (Edit → mark inactive), then add it again with Opening stock filled in. If the SKU/barcode is refused as "already used", clear them on the old product first.
  3. Last resort: Stock adjustment → Stock in, with reason "Opening stock". Stock and cost will be right, but remember that this month's P&L shows that value as a gain (lower write-offs).

5Unit cost: what to enter and how average cost works

Unit cost = what ONE unit actually cost you, not the selling price. This number drives your profit: profit on a sale = selling price − cost.

a) Opening stock unit cost

Enter what you paid per unit for the stock already on the shelf. Example: 10 back covers bought at ₹200 each → Unit cost 200. For IMEI phones with different costs, use the average (or add them as separate purchases, see below).

b) Purchase: the app calculates a "landed" cost

On a purchase you enter the vendor's price. The app then spreads discount, shipping and other charges over the units, including free units, to get the Landed / unit cost shown in the last column:

line total = qty × unit cost − line discount landed / unit = (line total [+ tax*] + share of shipping & other charges) ÷ (qty + free qty) * purchase tax is added to cost only when Settings → Tax says purchase tax is part of cost
Example: 2 × iPhone 16 (IMEI tracked) Unit cost ₹60,000 × 2 = ₹1,20,000, discount ₹1,000 → line total ₹1,19,000. Shipping ₹500.
Landed / unit = (1,19,000 + 500) ÷ 2 = ₹59,750 per phone. Enter both IMEIs on the line.

c) Weighted average cost: what the product remembers

Every time stock comes in, the app mixes the new cost with the old one:

new average = (qty on hand × old average + qty in × new unit cost) ÷ (qty on hand + qty in)
EventQtyUnit costStock afterAverage cost after
Opening stock: back covers+3₹150.003₹150.00

Sales and write-offs always take stock out at the current average cost. They never change the average.

d) IMEI items and cost

Each IMEI remembers the cost it was bought at, but profit uses the product's average. If you buy IMEI A at ₹59,750 and later IMEI B at ₹62,000, the product average becomes ₹60,875. Whichever phone you sell, the invoice records ₹60,875 as its cost. Over all sales the total profit is the same; only the split between individual invoices differs.
Tip: if one model's buying price varies a lot, create separate products (e.g. "iPhone 16 128GB Black") so each average stays meaningful.

7Stock adjustments: fixing count differences

Use these only when the physical shelf count differs from the app. Purchases, sales and returns already update stock automatically.

  1. Stock → Stock adjustment (or the adjust button on a product).
  2. Choose the product (not possible for IMEI-tracked items).
  3. Choose the type, enter quantity and a reason (required, e.g. "Monthly count – 1 missing").
  4. For Stock in only: optionally enter unit cost. Blank = current average cost (recommended).
  5. Save adjustment. A numbered adjustment and a stock movement are created and logged with your user name.

8How Profit & Loss is calculated

Open it at localhost:3000/reports/profit-loss and pick a date range. It works on the invoice date (accrual basis), not when cash was received.

Net revenue = sales (after discounts, excluding tax) − sales returns Direct costs = cost of goods sold + spare parts used in repairs + inventory write-offs Gross profit = net revenue − direct costs Net profit = gross profit − operating expenses − other expenses
  • Not in P&L: opening stock, purchases, paying vendors, customer credit payments. They move money or stock but are not profit or loss.
  • Tax collected is shown separately. It's owed to the government, not your income.
  • Cancelled invoices are excluded completely.

Wrong cost = wrong profit.If you enter opening stock with unit cost 0, every sale of that stock shows 100% profit. If you use "Stock in" for new stock, profit jumps by that stock's value. Always enter the real cost.

9Common mistakes & FAQ

I forgot opening stock when creating the product

See section 4 → "My product already exists with 0 stock".

I ticked Track IMEI on a back cover by mistake

If stock is 0: Edit the product, untick it and save. If stock is above 0, the checkbox is locked. You'd first need to bring stock to 0 (sell, return to vendor or cancel the purchase).

Why can't I adjust stock of an iPhone?

IMEI items can't be adjusted manually, so the count always matches the list of IMEIs. Use a purchase, sale, sales return or vendor return instead.

Why did the average cost change after a purchase?

Because new units at a different price were mixed in (see section 5c). That's expected.

Stock shows negative

Only happens if an admin sold beyond stock with the override. The next purchase brings it back up. Check Stock movements for the sale that caused it.

Should the "Purchase cost (reference)" field match my real cost?

It's only a reference and is updated automatically by purchases and opening stock. The real cost used in profit is the average cost column on the Stock page.
